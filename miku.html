The Quintessential Quintuplets

The third sister of the Nakano quintuplets. She is a shy and reserved girl with a passion for Sengoku period warlords. Usually seen wearing her trademark blue wireless headphones.

Though she started with low confidence, she worked hard on her cooking (especially baking) to express her feelings. Her love is quiet but incredibly deep and resilient.
